Breast screening combines mammogram evidence, structured biopsy/FNA measurements, and a radiologist's BI-RADS assessment. This project models that workflow as one decision-support platform rather than a single classifier.

A CNN pathway (VGG16, ResNet50, EfficientNet-B3/B4, and a custom lightweight CNN) classifies mammograms as benign or malignant with <Term id="explainability">Grad-CAM</Term> heatmaps; a separate tabular pathway runs the 30 structured features from the Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset schema through a multi-class BI-RADS classifier. A weighted risk-fusion step combines the CNN probability, the ML probability, and the BI-RADS category into `LOW_RISK`, `MODERATE_RISK`, or `HIGH_RISK`. FastAPI serves inference, risk scoring, and PDF report generation; a Streamlit dashboard provides Home, Analysis, and Dashboard views. Training data spans the WDBC tabular set and CBIS-DDSM mammogram imagery.

No model metrics or clinical validation results are published for this project yet.
