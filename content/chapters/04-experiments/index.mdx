These four projects are shorter studies rather than full case studies: each explores one technique or workflow end to end, without the multi-month scope of the systems in §3.

1. **§4.1 Image Captioning Benchmark** — comparing attention mechanisms for image captioning on Flickr8k.
2. **§4.2 Breast Cancer Diagnostic Platform** — multimodal screening decision support (research and educational use only).
3. **§4.3 Mars Surface Image Classifier and Downloader** — Curiosity rover imagery, classification and tooling.
4. **§4.4 Productivity Predictor Pipeline** — an end-to-end ML pipeline for garment-worker productivity.
