A downloader utility fetches Curiosity rover image URLs by Martian sol from NASA's Mars Photos API and saves them to a chosen output directory, with unit tests covering the downloader itself. The classifier trains a Keras model on a curated Mars image dataset (train/validation/test splits) drawn from the Mars Science Laboratory mission — Mastcam Right, Mastcam Left and MAHLI imagery, dataset reference DOI `10.5281/zenodo.1049137` — with notebook-based experimentation and evaluation, and a trained model artifact.

No classifier metrics are published for this project yet.
