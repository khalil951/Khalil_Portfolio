Four encoder-decoder captioners compared on Flickr8k, corpus BLEU on a 90/10 train/val split: a no-attention baseline, soft attention (<Cite id="xu2015" />), hard attention trained with <Term id="reinforce">REINFORCE</Term> and doubly stochastic regularization, and a Transformer encoder-decoder over a ResNet-50 encoder.

| Model | Final loss | Final BLEU | Best BLEU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Transformer (ResNet-50 encoder) | 1.6427 | 0.0792 | 0.0814 |
| SAT-Soft | 1.7797 | 0.0721 | 0.0721 |
| SAT-Hard | 2.0151 | 0.0667 | 0.0667 |
| Baseline (no attention) | 1.8917 | 0.0616 | 0.0616 |

Only BLEU is published for these runs — no METEOR or CIDEr numbers are shown, since none were measured.

The project grew into a small served app: the Transformer captioner behind FastAPI, fused with YOLOv8 object detection and EasyOCR text extraction, in front of a Streamlit UI with a pytest suite.
