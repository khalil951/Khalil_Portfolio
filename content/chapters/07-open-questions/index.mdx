## Why this book

Reading is one of my actual hobbies, not a device borrowed for this site — psychology and philosophy novels especially, and Dostoevsky is my favourite author. One line from *The Brothers Karamazov* has stuck with me: "The mystery of human existence lies not in just staying alive, but in finding something to live for." Framing this portfolio as a book — chapters, sections, an index — followed from that; it's the format I actually think in.

Outside of it: soccer, watching anime, writing on Substack, and listening to progressive rock.
