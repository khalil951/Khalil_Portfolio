These four systems are ordered the way the work itself progressed: most built and tested first, design-phase work after. Each one answers the same questions — what was the problem, what did I build, what tradeoffs did I choose, and what do the results actually show once measured and observed results are kept apart.

1. **§3.1 Xpress PPM Agent** — a guardrailed intake-triage agent for an enterprise project-portfolio tool, where deterministic rules override the LLM.
2. **§3.2 AgriSense AI** — a LangGraph supervisor deciding irrigation for date palms, grounded in real weather and soil data.
3. **§3.3 AI Research Assistant** — an agentic RAG app that fails closed toward honesty instead of guessing.
4. **§3.4 EstateMind** — a Tunisian real-estate intelligence platform, built as team lead on an ESPRIT capstone.
