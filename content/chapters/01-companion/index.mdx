## 1.2 What I work on

I build agentic and retrieval-augmented systems that are honest about what they know — from guardrailed enterprise agents to multi-agent decision systems. Two things show up in almost everything I ship: agents and RAG built end to end (LangGraph pipelines, guardrails, retrieval, APIs, UIs, tests), and engineering rigor paired with epistemic honesty — deterministic guardrails overriding the LLM where correctness matters more than fluency, and measured results kept visibly separate from observed behaviour.

## 1.3 How I think

<Term id="fail-closed">Fail closed toward honesty</Term>: I'd rather a system say "I don't know" than answer fluently and wrong. That principle runs through the case studies in §3 and §4 — <Term id="guardrails">deterministic guardrails</Term> that can override a model's output, and results sections that never blur what was actually measured into what was merely observed.
