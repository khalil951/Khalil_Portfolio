## AIESEC oGT Team Leader

oGT Team Leader at AIESEC in Tunisia (Bardo), 2023–2024: led a five-person team running outgoing-exchange participant operations and outreach, built a CRM system and a supply & demand dashboard, and coordinated with international AIESEC entities.

<Figure n="6.3" src="/images/accomplishments/aiesec.jpg" caption="An AIESEC in Tunisia (Bardo) outreach event, welcoming incoming exchange participants." />

## Global UGRAD Scholarship

Selected for the U.S. Department of State's Global UGRAD program — a fully-funded scholarship with roughly 200 students selected worldwide each year, 3 of them from Tunisia. The exchange semester itself, at the University of North Carolina at Greensboro, is covered in [§5.2 Foundations](/chapters/5).

<Figure n="6.4" src="/images/accomplishments/global-ugrad.jpg" caption="At the University of North Carolina at Greensboro, during the Global UGRAD exchange semester." />

## Volunteering

Community work alongside the roles above: volunteering with **Enactus**; with the **Tunisian Red Crescent** on blood-donation campaigns, disaster-relief efforts, and health-awareness programmes; and helping organise **International Student Week in Tunisia (ISWIT)** 2023, for more than 20 participants from diverse countries and cultural backgrounds.
