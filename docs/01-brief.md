# 01 — Portfolio Brief

## Goal
A portfolio that gets Khalil shortlisted for **AI Engineer / ML Engineer / LLM application** roles and research internships, by proving he can design and ship production-minded AI systems — not just train notebooks.

## Primary audiences (in priority order)
| Audience | Time spent | What they must find in < 30 seconds |
|---|---|---|
| Technical recruiter / hiring manager | 30–90 s | Role fit, strongest 3 projects, stack, location/availability, CV, contact |
| Engineer doing a technical screen | 3–10 min | Architecture decisions, tradeoffs, code links, tests, evaluation honesty |
| Research supervisor / program committee | 3–10 min | Depth of understanding, rigor, writing quality |
| Community / startup contacts | 1–2 min | Who he is, what he builds, how to reach him |

## Positioning statement (draft — confirm with Khalil)
> AI engineer building agentic and retrieval-augmented systems that are honest about what they know — from guardrailed enterprise agents to multi-agent decision systems. Data Science Engineering student at ESPRIT, Tunis.

Two proof pillars the whole site reinforces:
1. **Agents & RAG, built end-to-end** — LangGraph pipelines, guardrails, retrieval, APIs, UIs, tests.
2. **Engineering rigor & epistemic honesty** — deterministic guardrails overriding the LLM, fail-closed answer states, measured vs observed results kept separate.

## Success criteria
- CV and contact reachable in one click from any page.
- Every featured project answers: problem → approach → architecture → results → limitations → links.
- At least one interaction is itself a small demonstration of his AI skills ("Ask the portfolio", see `04-design-direction.md`).
- Nothing overclaims. A skeptical engineer should trust the site *more* after reading it.

## Out of scope for v1
- Blog CMS, comments, newsletter
- Multi-language (EN only; FR/AR later if useful)
- User accounts, admin dashboards

## Content that must NOT appear
- Ongoing job / internship / fellowship applications or interview prep
- Colleague or supervisor details beyond name + title, and only if Khalil approves
- Confidential client data — Xpress PPM visuals use the synthetic dataset only; check RFC's permission before publishing internal architecture diagrams
